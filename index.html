chord.dhtSPRING 2026

Distributed Chord Infrastructure

  • Nodes self-organize into a Chord DHT ring with SHA-1 consistent hashing and route requests in O(log N) hops, self-healing through stabilization.
  • Quorum replication to k successors with replica fallback on reads and layered retries with back-off.
  • REST and gRPC APIs, an LLM-assisted placement agent, Prometheus and Grafana, on AWS EC2 with Docker and Terraform. 100% job completion on a 7-node ring.
